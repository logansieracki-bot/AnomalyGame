ANOMALY SHIFT

12 AM - 6 AM. Watch the cameras. Report what is wrong. Don't let anything in.

  • Cameras: click the map (or keys 1-9). Only one camera at a time.
  • Report: select the room's camera, press REPORT, pick what is wrong. It takes a few seconds, then there is a cooldown.
  • Defend: lower the monitor (SPACE), then close the door (A) or vent (D) when you hear something arrive.
  • Not everything is real. Semi-transparent things are not reportable.
  • You lose if power runs out, overload fills, or something reaches you unblocked.
